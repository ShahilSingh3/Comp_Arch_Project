Marking Scheme: 
The marking is based on two aspects: register accuracy and counter accuracy.
Register accuracy: Per test case, you will either get 12 marks if all registers correct, or 3 marks (even if 1 register value is wrong). 5 test case marks will be averaged for getting the final marks for register accuracy.
Counter accuracy: Per test case, your marks will vary from 2 to 8. Here, the marks will be awarded linearly in that range depending on your accuracy, i.e., 0% accuracy -> 2 marks, 100% accuracy -> 8 marks, 50% accuracy -> 2 + (50/100)*(8-2) = 5 marks, and so on. Then, 5 test case marks will be averaged for getting the final marks for counter accuracy.
Finally, the two marks will be added and then it will be rounded off to the next integer as your mark in this assignment.
